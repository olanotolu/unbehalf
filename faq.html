Overview

What is Unbehalf?

Unbehalf is an ever-growing collection of creative, well-crafted JavaScript effects built with GSAP.

It offers interactive animations, text transitions, scroll experiences, mouse interactions, and reusable creative components designed for developers and designers looking to build polished web experiences faster.

The platform currently includes 112 effects, with new additions released every week, alongside resources like step-by-step tutorials, demos, downloadable ZIP file and Webflow-ready component.

What’s part of the subscription plan?

You’ll get access to the platform, currently featuring 112 effects, plus new additions released every week, alongside step-by-step tutorials, demos, downloadable ZIP files, and Webflow-ready components.

Can I explore the platform before subscribing?

Yes! You can try our free effects. We created them in collaboration with the GSAP and Codrops teams — no credit card required.

Do I need the Gsap premium plan to use your effects?

No, since April 2025, GSAP has been completely free.

Do you offer a Webflow-ready version of each effect?

Absolutely, Copy-to-Webflow buttons are available for members. Paste any effect directly into your Webflow page — no complicated setup, no script headaches, just copy and go!

Do the effects work on mobile devices?

Yes, the effects are designed to work on mobile devices. In some cases, we disable or adapt them on mobile if needed for performance or usability, but the goal is always to make them work smoothly across devices.

Can I contact support if I have an issue?

Absolutely! If you run into any issues with the code, don’t hesitate to reach out to us. You can reach us via the support method in the logged-in menu.

I bought the first batch — will I keep access forever?

Yes! As promised, the first batch is yours forever. Whether you subscribe in the future or not, you’ll always keep access to it.

Usage & Licensing

Can I use the effects in personal or commercial projects?

Yes! You’re free to use the effects, resources, and code snippets in your own projects—whether personal or commercial. You can also modify or adapt the code to fit your needs. For more details, check out our permissions page.

Can I sell themes that include these effects (WordPress, Framer, Webflow, etc.)?

No, you cannot sell themes that include the effects and code snippets. They cannot be resold, redistributed, or included in any pre-built themes or templates, whether free or paid. This includes WordPress themes, Framer and Webflow templates, or any other marketplace or distribution method. You also cannot use them in any course content. For more details, please visit our permissions page.

Can I include these effects in open-source repositories (e.g., GitHub, GitLab)?

No, you cannot include the effects or code snippets in any open-source repositories. Making them publicly available in open-source projects counts as redistribution, which is not permitted under our license. For more details, please visit our permissions page.

Can I still use the effects after canceling my subscription?

Of course. Even after canceling your subscription, you can continue using the effects in any of your projects. You’ll simply lose access to the platform and to future/new effects included with the subscription.

If you purchased the first batch of effects (000–050), you’ll keep permanent access to those effects — whether you have an active subscription or not.

How can I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. You’ll retain access until the end of your quarterly or annual subscription period. After that, access to the platform will expire.

To cancel your subscription, simply go to Account > Manage Subscription. You’ll be redirected to Stripe, where you can manage or cancel your subscription.

Account & Billing

How much does Unbehalf cost?

Unbehalf is available through quarterly or annual subscriptions.

This gives you full access to all effects, including every new effects released during your subscription period.

What payment methods do you accept?

All payments are securely processed through Stripe. With Stripe, you can easily purchase Unbehalf using your credit card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Stripe is one of the most trusted payment platforms worldwide, ensuring a safe and reliable transaction.

Do you offer refunds?

Unfortunately, since you have access to the file download automatically after a transaction is processed, we can’t offer refunds. That said, if you have any issues, don’t hesitate to reach our support.

Can I change my plan later?

Not during your current subscription period. You’ll need to wait until your subscription ends before switching plans.

Will my subscription price ever change?

Once you subscribe, your pricing stays the same for as long as your membership remains active.

As Unbehalf grows and new features are added, pricing may evolve over time for new members — but existing members will always keep their original rate.

Is there a discount for students?

Yes, we do offer a student discount. Simply contact us with a valid student ID or any proof of enrollment to receive your discount.

How do I update my billing information?

You can easily update all your billing information in your account settings. Rest assured, none of this information is stored in our database, so your security is never compromised.

Can I share my account with a co-worker or friend?

No — each account is intended for a single user only.

If we detect account sharing or any misuse of the platform, we may temporarily suspend access until the issue has been resolved.

Is my personal information secure?

Of course! We only store a few pieces of information in our database: your email and password, which are encrypted using web-standard security measures. We don’t store any payment details, so everything is fully protected.
