A Model of One’s Own

Introducing Unbehalf

— An illustration of A Room of One’s Own by Virginia Woolf, 1929.

There’s nothing quite like feeling understood — a sibling who finishes your sentences, a friend who hears “I’m fine” and knows you are not. The people who know us best can help us most, because they see beneath what we say to what we really mean. That takes years of accumulated knowledge: a model of you — not just emails and messages, but who you are, what you do, your people, preferences, goals, commitments, and intent.

The same will be true for personal software as software becomes agents. The next leap in how we interact with machines will be unlocked by infrastructure that serves full, up-to-the-minute context on us — efficiently, securely, and ubiquitously. That infrastructure is missing. Agents can now watch, learn, and distil our behaviour into reusable context on an always-on basis. But who does the watching, who owns the model that results, and what happens to our privacy in exchange?

Your Unbehalf, and the model of you

Unbehalf is self-sovereign infrastructure that autonomously builds and serves your portable user model across your devices and agents. It begins as a streaming-state, portable context vault that builds itself: background agents continuously learn from your activity, up to the minute, with no action needed from you. Over time it builds a full model of you — goals, priorities, style, preferences, commitments, and intent — which you can serve over local and cloud APIs or MCP, with your full control. The API of you, for humans and machines alike. Next time you ask, there’s no need to explain: Unbehalf was already with you.

Privacy and sovereignty are paramount

A model of you is like a home. It holds your most personal things; you decide who comes in, what they see, and when they leave. Whoever holds our user models will steer what software does for us — and every company’s priorities are, naturally, different from yours. Putting control in the hands of the people these models describe will shape the industry’s incentives for the better.

  • Your model is yours, truly. Unbehalf runs on your device by default and lives in a folder on your computer — inspect it, edit it, back it up, or move it to a USB stick.
  • Bring-your-own intelligence. Power Unbehalf with an existing Claude or ChatGPT subscription, your own API key, local inference, or private inference tokens.
  • We stay off the path. Unbehalf does not see your data; it never passes through us unless you choose hosted private inference or Unbehalf Cloud. No training on it, no logging it, no selling it.
  • Confidential, from day one. Zero data retention is supported, with confidential computing and verifiable controls wherever possible — including confidential inference and, if you enable it, a confidential VM for Unbehalf Cloud.
  • You can run it all. Key components of Unbehalf are intended to be open-sourced, so you can self-host them on your laptop or in the cloud.

Who we are

Unbehalf is an applied research and product company studying how AI can understand us deeply, how to protect that understanding, and how we can use it to empower us to do great things. As agents come to power all the software we use, your personal context will become ever more precious. With Unbehalf, you can create and run your own user model — and permission access to your agents in a way that feels intuitive.
